### How to use

1. Fill this file in your editor
2. Go to https://your-preview-url/forge
3. Paste the raw text into the "Drop business info — raw" zone at top
4. Click Parse & autofill → then Forge
5. Your site will be live at `/sites/{your-slug}` and saved in vault `/projects`
6. Custom repo created at `sites/{slug}/forge.json` — ownable, deployable

### What WebForge does with it

- Parses narrative for motifs, mood, audience
- Picks palette from vibe (paper, ink, accent)
- Generates typography (Instrument Serif + Geist)
- Forges full Next.js 14 page with Fable 5 motion
- Creates custom repository folder you own
- No lock-in, no watermark
